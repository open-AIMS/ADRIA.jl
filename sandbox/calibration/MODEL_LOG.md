# COTS calibration model log

This is the curated decision ledger for the Lizard/Owen peak-and-crash work, not a replacement for immutable run directories. The generated [run inventory](MODEL_RUN_INVENTORY.csv) indexes archived non-hidden run directories, including incomplete and unreviewed runs; its raw metadata status is **not** a promotion decision. Regenerate it with `python sandbox/calibration/build_model_run_inventory.py` after adding a run. Each row below names the archived evidence, what was actually varied, and what the result does **not** establish. Add a dated row before interpreting a new screen; retain failed and superseded runs. The frozen three-year-smoothed manta objective, reef-area aggregation, and provisional CPUE conversion are described in [README.md](README.md). No mechanism below is promoted to the default model.

## Current diagnosis and next discriminating test

The 1991 full-cohort anchor gets Lizard's first-wave timing, but modeled adults survive and graze through the gap. Merely withholding grazing raises coral and also supports the surviving COTS. Existing juvenile storage and higher **constant** adult mortality failed on this exact anchor. Stage storage retained juveniles during 2004–12 (Lizard mean `0→0.309 COTS/ha/year`) but raised rather than lowered Lizard's gap adult density (`1.028→1.126 COTS/ha`), and coral in 2012 fell (`0.093→0.078`). The lagged density/food adult hazard also failed because its trigger was active around the first wave but almost absent by 2008–12. The exact-control cohort audit now shows that Lizard's 2008–12 floor is mostly *adult carryover*, not just maturation, while externally supplied recruits replenish the later pipeline. The sawtooth in 1999–2007 reflects oscillating food limitation. The next structural question is whether a duration-gated, density-triggered post-peak loss can remove standing adults **and** avoid rapid cohort refill while allowing later reseeding. A ReefMod-style timer is eligible as an explicitly phenomenological, opt-in diagnostic benchmark, not as evidence that pathogens caused the observed crash. Keep the independently supported Allee threshold fixed. The equations and gate are in [crash mechanism protocol](crash_mechanism_protocol.md).

**Update 2026-10-09.** The preregistered preferred-prey starvation × senescence screen supersedes the timer as the next step, and it also failed. It localised two structural constraints:

1. **Linear grazing.** With linear grazing and `a_F=1.18`, the anchor eliminates fast coral in its first year. Legacy food survival (total cover) therefore reflects massives only. With starvation correctly tied to preferred coral, COTS are pinned at a low density that keeps fast coral just under the threshold.
2. **Settlement floor.** Senescence lowers the trough but cannot remove the gap floor, because the gap adults are young recruits from continuing internal/external settlement.

The next structural test is preferred-first, saturating or per-capita consumption combined with preferred-prey starvation and senescence. Running it first requires an explicit decision on the density unit, because the frozen `0.2942 COTS/tow` per model adult/ha scale implies about 20 field COTS/ha per model unit versus `q≈0.015`.

**Update 2026-10-09 (unit migration).** The project lead approved fixing the observation unit at `q=0.015 COTS/tow per COTS/ha`, a convention shared with CoCoNet and ReefMod rather than a measured conversion. Under consistent units (v2, RNG pinned), per-capita grazing combined with preferred-prey starvation and senescence is the first configuration whose dynamics have the observed form: outbreak, starvation crash, coral recovery. It still fails the gate on second-wave presence (MacGillivray, North Direction) and on first-wave timing. Calibration runs that compare parameter values must pin `ADRIA_RNG_SEED`.
